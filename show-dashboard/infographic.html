One proof layer for six kinds of work.

What changes, and what does not

Each team works with different sources and makes different decisions. Tracemark preserves that context, creates a fingerprint of the resulting record, anchors a compact proof to Hedera, and makes the record available for checking through Mirror Node.

Shared proof layer

Every team keeps its own workflow while using the same way to verify a record.

Each workflow keeps its own context, while the proof layer gives every record the same path to a public verification.

01 / sourceReadThe team captures an external source or action.
02 / eventNormalizeThe input becomes a consistent event.
03 / fingerprintSHA-256The event receives a deterministic fingerprint.
04 / anchorHCSA compact proof is published to Hedera.
05 / verifyMirror NodeThe record is checked against Mirror Node.
